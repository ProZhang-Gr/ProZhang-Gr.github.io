IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics · 2025

Prediction of circRNA-Drug Associations Based on Bipartite Graph Transformer

Zihan Zhang, Yuchen Zhang*, Xiujuan Lei

A dual-pathway graph framework for circRNA-drug association prediction: a GAT encodes homogeneous subgraphs while a Bipartite Graph Transformer captures heterogeneous high-order interactions, fused under uncertainty weighting.

  • Triple topological positional encoding: absolute degree + degree rank + spectral encoding
  • AUC ≈ 0.967 on Resistance; cold-start AUC ≈ 0.95+ for unseen circRNAs

Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling · 2026

NCRDLLM: Predicting ncRNA-Drug Response Associations via Multimodal Feature Fusion and Large Language Models

Zihan Zhang, Yuchen Zhang*

A unified large-language-model framework covering lncRNA, miRNA and circRNA. Six biological modalities are mapped into the LLaMA-3.2-3B latent space via lightweight feature adapters and fine-tuned with LoRA.

  • 6-modality FeatureAdapter → LLaMA-3.2-3B (LoRA r=64, α=64) → Weighted Pooling
  • One framework for three ncRNA types; disease modality bridges cold-start cases

A little more about me

My research life began in early 2025. I completed all the work independently — both papers are sole first-author. I'm deeply grateful to my advisor, Prof. Yuchen Zhang, for his tremendous support.

🧭How I work

Planning over execution. Once I commit to a direction I enjoy, I immerse myself in it — sometimes thinking aloud until I find an explanation I can believe. I'm good at planning time and tasks, habitually summarize each stage, and rarely feel rushed.

🎨Personality & taste

Outgoing, and confident in social settings — the hackathon team I led reached 4th in the finals, and we became close friends. I believe in ‘play hard, study hard.’ I enjoy color and photography, and play badminton.

🚀On the future

I lean toward a direct PhD — five continuous years of deep work. My focus is the AI side rather than the SCI side: exploring LLMs/Agents as the ‘brain.’ I've been systematically studying LLM foundations (MiniMind, Stanford CS336, Hello-Agent, RL). I'm especially drawn to Zhongguancun Academy in Beijing for its youth and fresh vision.

🤝What I hope for in an advisor

‘Serious in research, lively in life.’ Someone who can offer guidance when I'm lost, and genuinely share in the joy of my achievements.

“Among any three people, one can be my teacher.” Every subfield of AI has its own value, and I'd love to hear your story.What has driven you this far — and why did you choose this path? If you'd be willing to talk, I'll treasure the chance.
